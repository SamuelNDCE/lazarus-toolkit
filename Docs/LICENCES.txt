MALWAREBYTES              tagged "licence" in the launcher
----------------------------------------------------------------
  THE FREE VERSION IS NOT LICENSED FOR BUSINESS DEVICES.
  Malwarebytes' own wording: the free product "is not licensed for
  threat removal on business devices", and they say they detect
  business environments and act on it.

  THERE IS NO FREE PORTABLE MALWAREBYTES.
  The portable one is the MALWAREBYTES TOOLSET, 18 tools that run
  off a USB stick with nothing installed on the customer's
  machine. That is gated behind TechBench ADVANCED.

  TECHBENCH, ACCURATELY:
    BASIC     free for any repair shop owner. Reseller margins up
              to 25%, and a 15-DAY TRIAL of the Toolset. That is a
              trial, not ongoing access.
    ADVANCED  apply for it. Margins up to 70%, FULL access to all
              18 portable Toolset tools, and 25 Malwarebytes
              licences to resell included on joining.
    Apply: malwarebytes.com/techbench

  So: MBSetup.exe on this stick INSTALLS Malwarebytes, it is not
  portable. If you want the plug-in-and-scan workflow, Advanced is
  the route, and it is a reseller programme rather than a plain
  subscription, so the 25 licences may cover the cost.

  AdwCleaner IS free and IS portable, and is also Malwarebytes.
  It covers adware and PUPs rather than full malware.
