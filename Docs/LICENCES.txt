================================================================
 LICENCE NOTES  -  the tools with strings attached
================================================================

 Three separate questions get asked about every tool here, and
 they have different answers. Conflating them is how people get
 this wrong.

   1. MAY I USE IT ON PAID WORK?
      Some free tiers are personal or home use only.

   2. MAY I CARRY THE BINARY ON THIS STICK?
      Redistribution is a different permission from use. A tool
      can be free for commercial use and still forbid you
      passing the binary on.

   3. MAY I NAME IT IN THE README?
      Almost always yes. Naming a product to say what it does is
      nominative use and is not a licence question.

 THE REPO ITSELF REDISTRIBUTES NOTHING. .gitignore excludes
 *.exe, *.msi, *.zip, *.7z, *.iso and Tools/* contents, so a
 clone carries our scripts and nothing of anyone else's. Every
 tool is fetched from its own publisher. Question 2 therefore
 only applies to a physical stick, and only if that stick is
 handed to somebody else.

 Dates below are when each entry was last checked against the
 vendor's own page. Undated means carried over unverified.

SYSINTERNALS              Autoruns, Process Explorer,
                          Process Monitor, TCPView
  Checked 2026-08-17 against the Sysinternals Software License
  Terms on Microsoft Learn.
----------------------------------------------------------------
  ONE LICENCE COVERS ALL FOUR. A verdict on Autoruns is a
  verdict on Process Explorer, Procmon and TCPView.

  USE: no commercial restriction. The only use carve-out is
  "commercial software hosting services", which is not what a
  repair job is.

  INSTALL RIGHT, VERBATIM: "You may install and use any number
  of copies of the software on YOUR DEVICES." A client's laptop
  is not your device. Running these on somebody else's machine
  is not clearly granted.

  REDISTRIBUTION: forbidden, in four separate clauses. You may
  not "publish the software for others to copy", "rent, lease or
  lend the software", or "transfer the software or this
  agreement to any third party".

  WHAT THAT MEANS HERE:
    Carrying them on your own stick for your own use is fine.
    Handing that stick to somebody else is not.
    Leaving a copy behind on a client machine is not.

  THE CLEAN ROUTE, if this ever matters: live.sysinternals.com
  serves every tool over the network from Microsoft's own
  server. You run it, you never hold a copy, and the transfer
  is Microsoft's rather than yours.

  NOTE: a previous version of this file listed Sysinternals
  under "no strings". That was wrong.


OCCT                      tagged "licence" in the launcher
  Checked 2026-08-17 against the OCBASE EULA.
----------------------------------------------------------------
  THE FREE LICENCE IS PERSONAL USE ONLY. Their wording: a free
  licence to use the Software "for your personal use only to the
  exclusion of any commercial purposes in any form whatsoever".

  They also say plainly that unauthorised commercial use "can be
  subject to legal prosecution".

  Paid client work needs the Premium licence, via Patreon or
  direct with OCBASE. OCBASE is a French company, so this is
  enforceable in the EU rather than theoretical.

  Stress testing a machine you are being paid to repair is
  commercial use. This is the sharpest restriction on the stick.


MALWAREBYTES              tagged "licence" in the launcher
  Checked 2026-08-12.
----------------------------------------------------------------
  THE FREE VERSION IS NOT LICENSED FOR BUSINESS DEVICES.
  Malwarebytes' own wording: the free product "is not licensed
  for threat removal on business devices", and they say they
  detect business environments and act on it.

  THERE IS NO FREE PORTABLE MALWAREBYTES. The portable one is
  the MALWAREBYTES TOOLSET, 18 tools that run off a USB stick
  with nothing installed on the customer's machine. That is
  gated behind TechBench ADVANCED.

  TECHBENCH, ACCURATELY:
    BASIC     free for any repair shop owner. Reseller margins
              up to 25%, and a 15-DAY TRIAL of the Toolset.
              That is a trial, not ongoing access.
    ADVANCED  apply for it. Margins up to 70%, FULL access to
              all 18 portable Toolset tools, and 25 Malwarebytes
              licences to resell included on joining.
    Apply: malwarebytes.com/techbench

  So: MBSetup.exe on this stick INSTALLS Malwarebytes, it is not
  portable. If you want the plug-in-and-scan workflow, Advanced
  is the route, and it is a reseller programme rather than a
  plain subscription, so the 25 licences may cover the cost.

  AdwCleaner IS free and IS portable, and is also Malwarebytes.
  It covers adware and PUPs rather than full malware.

GPU-Z
  Checked 2026-08-17 against the TechPowerUp GPU-Z page.
----------------------------------------------------------------
  USE: "free to use for personal and commercial usage." No
  problem on paid work.

  REDISTRIBUTION: "you may not redistribute GPU-Z as part of a
  commercial package." Lazarus is Apache 2.0 and is not sold,
  so listing and launching it is fine. Selling a stick with
  GPU-Z on it would not be.


FIREFOX
  Checked 2026-08-17 against Mozilla's Distribution Policy.
----------------------------------------------------------------
  Distributing UNALTERED copies needs no permission. Altering
  them and keeping the name needs written consent.

  Mozilla count as alterations: adding extensions, CHANGING
  DEFAULT SETTINGS, altering search codes, file location
  changes, modifying the installer.

  WATCH THIS ONE. Lazarus runs Firefox with its profile kept on
  the stick, which is a settings change, and "Firefox-Portable"
  builds are generally repackaged by third parties rather than
  shipped by Mozilla. Fine for your own use. Do not hand the
  stick to anyone else with a modified Firefox still called
  Firefox on it.

HIREN'S BOOTCD PE
----------------------------------------------------------------
  Built on Windows 11 PE, which is Microsoft licensed. The
  project states it contains only free and legal software, but
  the PE base is not theirs to relicense and is not ours to
  redistribute. Obtain it from hirensbootcd.org only. Never
  commit it, and it stays out of any stick handed to a third
  party.

================================================================
 THE SHORT VERSION
================================================================
  Nothing here is a lawsuit waiting to happen while the stick
  stays yours and the repo ships no binaries. Both of those are
  currently true.

  The three that would actually bite:
    OCCT          personal use only, and they say they will act
    Sysinternals  yours to use, not yours to pass on
    Malwarebytes  free tier is not for business devices

  This is a reading of published licence text, not legal advice.
  If Lazarus ever ships as a product, or a stick is sold or
  given to a third party, that is the point to involve a
  solicitor rather than a checklist.
